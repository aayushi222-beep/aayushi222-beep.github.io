About

Who I am

I am a final-year Aerospace Engineering undergraduate at VIT Bhopal, specialising in high-speed unsteady aerodynamics, control surface optimisation, and small satellite propulsion. My work sits at the intersection of rigorous theory and hands-on experimentation.

My capstone research at the Unsteady Aerodynamics Laboratory, IIT Kanpur — under Prof. Debopam Das — investigates the autorotation of Shorea herryana seeds, combining high-speed imaging, Particle Image Velocimetry, and analytical modelling. In parallel, I interned at the Aeronautical Development Agency (Ministry of Defence), where I developed MATLAB frameworks for longitudinal stability analysis on combat aircraft.

I co-developed Project AMRUT, a water-based resistojet propulsion system for CubeSats, which I presented at the Global Space Exploration Conference (GLEX 2025). The project was also showcased at IAC 2024 and CEAS–AIDAA 2025. I am now applying for Masters programmes in aerospace and fluid dynamics to continue this trajectory at a graduate research level.

Projects

What I've built

01 Project AMRUT Propulsion
+
A water-based resistojet propulsion system designed for CubeSat-class spacecraft — safe, non-toxic, and low-power.
  • Designed propulsion architecture: dual pressurised tanks, coil-based resistive heating, vaporisation chamber, and nozzle optimised for <0.4U volume.
  • Performed theoretical sizing and CFD-based validation of mass flow rate, power consumption, exit velocity, and thrust — achieving close agreement between analytical and numerical predictions.
  • Presented at IAC 2024 (Milan), GLEX 2025, and CEAS–AIDAA 2025 as co-author and presenter.

03 FRS — Flight Recorder System Avionics
+
A modular, low-cost flight recorder inspired by aircraft black boxes, enabling real-time RF telemetry.
