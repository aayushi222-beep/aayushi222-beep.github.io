01 — About

Who I am

I am a final-year Aerospace Engineering undergraduate at VIT Bhopal, specialising in high-speed unsteady aerodynamics, control surface optimisation, and small satellite propulsion. My work sits at the intersection of rigorous theory and hands-on experimentation.

My capstone research at the Unsteady Aerodynamics Laboratory, IIT Kanpur — under Prof. Debopam Das — investigates the autorotation of Shorea herryana seeds, combining high-speed imaging, Particle Image Velocimetry, and analytical modelling to understand how nature achieves remarkably stable descent at low Reynolds numbers.

I interned at the Aeronautical Development Agency (Ministry of Defence), developing MATLAB frameworks for longitudinal stability analysis on combat aircraft. I also co-developed Project AMRUT, a water-based resistojet for CubeSats, presented at GLEX 2025, IAC 2024, and CEAS–AIDAA 2025.

02 — Research

Laboratory Work

IIT Kanpur — Unsteady Aerodynamics Laboratory
Aerodynamics of Autorotating Seeds — Shorea herryana
Supervisor: Prof. Debopam Das  ·  Jul 2025 – Present
  • Conducted seed drop tests using high-speed cameras to measure vertical and angular velocities, estimating drag coefficients from equations of motion.
  • Built a low-speed laminar gust generator to study seed stability; characterised flow profile with a pitot tube.
  • Measured aspect ratio and twist angle for fabricated scaled-up Mylar models; validated drag measurements against drop-test results.
  • Performed Particle Image Velocimetry (PIV) in wind tunnel to characterise vortex shedding at higher speeds. Vortex shedding observed and characterised at the leeward side of the petal.
  • Developed drag model using propeller lifting line theory; validated flow profiles using PIV.
